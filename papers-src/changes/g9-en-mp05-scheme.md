# g9-en-mp05-scheme
Source: new marking scheme written for g9-en-mp05-paper (no original).
## Flags (decisions taken on ambiguous questions)
- Part I A Q9: "straight line AB" read as an infinite line, giving two parallel lines 3 cm either side. If AB is a segment the locus is a "stadium" (two parallel segments plus two semicircles of radius 3 cm at the ends). Markers may accept the latter.
- Part I A Q2: answer 1111 (base two); 01111 also accepted.
- Part I B Q1 (scale drawing): theoretical values: FT = 30 tan 40 = 25.17 m (5.03 cm on the drawing), PT = 30 / cos 40 = 39.16 m (7.83 cm). Accepted ranges: FT 24-26 m, PT 38-40 m. Angle of depression = 40 degrees (alternate angles).
- Part II Q2(c): 4 x 10^4 is already in scientific notation; the two marks are 1 for the quotient 4 and the power 10^4, 1 for the standard form.
- Part II Q3 (iii): paper asks to read the graph for 3.5 km; exact value 80 x 3.5 + 100 = Rs 380 (accept 375-385 from a drawn graph). The table in Q3(i) has y = 180, 340, 420 missing.
- Part II Q5: constructed 120 degrees; foot D lies on YX produced beyond X, so XD = 5 cos 60 = 2.5 cm and ZD = 5 sin 60 = 4.33 cm (accept 2.4-2.6 and 4.2-4.4).
- Part II Q6: BC = 10 m (6-8-10), perimeter 52 m, grass area 97.5 m2, cost Rs 7 800. Flower bed (diameter 7 m) fits inside the 8 m high garden.
- Part II Q7(a)(iv): 4/5; (b): fifth number 13, new mean 13.
## Notes
- Mark splits follow the marks printed on the paper: each Part I B / Part II question totals 10.
- Diagrams (fig-1 scale drawing, fig-2 Venn, fig-3 graph, fig-4 construction result) generated as SVG.
