Part A

Answer only five questions.

1.

Nimesha and Ruwan each buy the same water pump for their gardens, whose cash price is Rs. 78 000. Nimesha takes a bank loan of Rs. 78 000 and settles it with a single payment at the end of one year, paying Rs. 10 140 as interest. Ruwan buys the pump on hire purchase: he pays 12 equal monthly instalments of Rs. 7 345 at the end of each month. Each instalment includes Rs. 6 500 of the price of the pump, and the total interest of Rs. 10 140 is calculated on the balance of the price still owed (the reducing balance). Show that the annual interest rate of Ruwan’s method is higher than that of Nimesha’s method, by answering the following.

(i)
Find the annual interest rate (as a percentage) of Nimesha’s loan.
(Marks 2)
(ii)
Write the balance of the price owed by Ruwan at the start of the 1st, 2nd and 12th months.
(Marks 2)
(iii)
The 12 monthly balances form an arithmetic progression. Find the sum of these 12 balances.
(Marks 2)
(iv)
The interest for one month on a balance of B rupees at r% per annum is B × r100 × 12. Using the sum in (iii), find the annual interest rate r of Ruwan’s method.
(Marks 3)
(v)
Hence show that the rate of Ruwan’s method is higher than that of Nimesha’s method.
(Marks 1)
