Part A

Answer only five questions. (10 marks each)

1.

Lahiru plans to buy a new oven for his bakery. The cash price of the oven is Rs 96 000. A finance company offers two methods of payment, each over a period of six months.

Method 1: Take a loan of Rs 96 000 and settle it, together with the total interest of Rs 10 080, in one payment at the end of the six months.

Method 2: Repay the Rs 96 000 in 6 equal monthly parts of capital, one at the end of each month. Interest is charged every month on the balance owed at the beginning of that month (reducing balance). The total interest paid over the six months is also Rs 10 080.

(i)
Find the annual interest rate of Method 1.
(2 marks)
(ii)
(a) Write down the balance owed at the beginning of each of the six months, and find the sum of these six balances.
(3 marks)
(b) Using this, find the annual interest rate of Method 2.
(3 marks)
(iii)
Show that Method 2 has the higher annual interest rate, and give a reason for this.
(2 marks)
