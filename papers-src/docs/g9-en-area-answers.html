Formulas: Parallelogram = base × height  •  Trapezium = ½(a + b)h  •  Circle = πr2

Question 1

(i) Area = base × height = 14 × 6 = 84 cm2

(ii) Perimeter = 2(14 + 8) = 2 × 22 = 44 cm

(iii) Area = BC × h → 84 = 8 × h → h = 84 ÷ 8 = 10.5 cm

Question 2

(i) 96 = 12 × h → h = 96 ÷ 12 = 8 cm

(ii) 2(PQ + QR) = 40 → PQ + QR = 20 → QR = 20 − 12 = 8 cm

(iii) Area = QR × distance → 96 = 8 × d → d = 12 cm

Question 3

(i) Area = ½(9 + 15) × 8 = ½ × 24 × 8 = 96 cm2

(ii) length × 8 = 96 → length = 12 cm

(iii) New area = ½(9 + 18) × 8 = ½ × 27 × 8 = 108 cm2; increase = 108 − 96 = 12 cm2

Question 4

(i) 70 = ½(6 + 8) × h → 70 = 7h → h = 10 cm

(ii) 80 = ½(5 + b) × 10 → 80 = 5(5 + b) → 5 + b = 16 → b = 11 cm

(iii) 70 = ½(a + b) × 10 → a + b = 14. Any two pairs, e.g. 4 cm and 10 cm, 5 cm and 9 cm (also 3 & 11, 2 & 12, 6 & 8, …)

Question 5

(i) Area = πr2 = 227 × 7 × 7 = 154 cm2

(ii) r = 28 ÷ 2 = 14 cm; Area = 227 × 14 × 14 = 616 cm2

(iii) 227 × r2 = 1386 → r2 = 1386 × 7 ÷ 22 = 441 → r = 21 cm (radius cannot be negative)

Question 6

(i) Area of square = 14 × 14 = 196 cm2

(ii) Diameter of circle = side of square = 14 cm, so radius = 7 cm

(iii) Area of circle = 227 × 7 × 7 = 154 cm2

(iv) Remaining area = 196 − 154 = 42 cm2

Question 7
